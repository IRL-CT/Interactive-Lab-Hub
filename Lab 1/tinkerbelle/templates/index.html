Leave manual off for the real demo: the fireflies find each other on their own. Turn it on to drive each flash by hand, or tap a firefly's screen to flash it at any time. 0 = fireflies ignore each other and never sync. At the default 0.05 two phones take roughly 40–55s to come together; 0.10 cuts that to about 10s. Below 0.03 they often never lock at all. How much each firefly retunes its own natural rhythm toward what it hears. Needed for full sync from very different starting speeds.
